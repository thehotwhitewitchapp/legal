Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

The Hot White Witch app respects your personal privacy. This document outlines how your data is processed, stored, and protected when you interact with our mobile application.

1. Data Collection

We receive minimal personal information required to facilitate core mobile features:

  • Account Credentials: Email address and display name utilized for secure user authentication.
  • Application Engagement: User selected intention categories, audio playback records, favorite tracks, and daily tarot draws utilized to persist your custom settings across devices.
  • Billing Validation: Anonymous receipt tokens and subscription status indicators supplied by Apple App Store and Google Play Billing. We never collect or retain primary payment method credentials.

2. Purpose of Processing

Collected information is used exclusively to maintain active accounts, restore prior digital purchases, present customized audio libraries, track feature reliability, and deliver software optimizations.

3. Cloud Infrastructure Partners

We work exclusively with recognized security verified infrastructure providers to run application services:

  • Google Cloud and Firebase: Provides encrypted user authentication and real time database storage.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: Processes subscription entitlement verifications and platform payment flows securely.

4. Account Deletion and Rights

You maintain complete ownership of your personal profile. You may permanently delete your account and wipe all stored application records directly within the in application settings screen or by submitting a written request to customer support.

5. Minor Protection

The application is intended for individuals of legal age. We do not knowingly target or collect personal identifying records from anyone under thirteen years of age.

6. Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding data privacy safeguards, please reach out to us at thehotwhitewitch@gmail.com.
